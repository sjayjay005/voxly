Voxly – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026 (v1.1)

Voxly is a Chrome extension that reads text on web pages aloud. This policy explains what happens to your data.

What Voxly does not do

  • Voxly does not collect, store, sell or share any personal information.
  • Voxly has no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no accounts.
  • Voxly does not track your browsing history.

Page text

When you ask Voxly to read something (via the toolbar button, right-click menu or keyboard shortcut), it reads the selected text or the main article text of the current tab and passes it to Chrome's built-in text-to-speech engine. The text is not saved or sent anywhere by Voxly.

Note: if you choose one of Chrome's online voices (marked "(online)" in the voice list, such as the "Google" voices), Chrome itself sends the text to Google's speech service to produce audio. That is handled by Chrome under Google's Privacy Policy. Choose an offline voice if you prefer text to stay on your device.

Neural voices (optional)

If you switch on neural voices, Voxly downloads the open-source Kokoro voice model files once from Hugging Face (huggingface.co) and caches them in your browser. Speech is then generated entirely on your computer: the text being read is never sent to Hugging Face or anywhere else.

Settings

Your chosen voice, speed and pitch are stored with Chrome's sync storage so they follow your Chrome profile. They contain no personal information.
